Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly to track expenses, we store the data you enter: amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not track your activity across other websites.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — to display your expense history, generate summaries, and power the features you use.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated expense data is permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces for the interface. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. No personal expense data is shared with Google or any other third party.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information available on our site. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
